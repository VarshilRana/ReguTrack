The regulatory challenge

Important information is often spread across disconnected workflows.

Regulatory teams work with evolving guidelines, product registrations and extensive technical documentation. Keeping these elements aligned can make it difficult to understand which products or dossier sections may need attention after a regulatory change.

Scattered regulatory updates Guidelines and notifications can be difficult to monitor consistently.
Complex product records Registration details and supporting information need clear organization.
Extensive CTD documentation Large dossier structures require traceable document management.
Unclear change impact Teams need to assess whether a new requirement affects existing products or documents.

Structured technical documentation

Understand the five modules of a CTD dossier.

ReguTrack organizes technical submission content around the Common Technical Document structure, helping teams locate and manage information consistently.

01 Regional

Administrative Information

Region-specific administrative and prescribing information for a regulatory submission.

  • Application forms
  • Product information
  • Regional requirements
  • Administrative documents
02 Summaries

CTD Summaries

High-level summaries and overviews of the quality, nonclinical and clinical data.

  • Quality overall summary
  • Nonclinical overview
  • Clinical overview
  • Summary documents
03 Quality

Quality Documentation

Detailed information about the drug substance, drug product, manufacturing and quality controls.

  • Manufacturing process
  • Specifications and methods
  • Stability data
  • Quality controls
04 Nonclinical

Nonclinical Study Reports

Nonclinical pharmacology, pharmacokinetic and toxicology study reports.

  • Pharmacology studies
  • Pharmacokinetics
  • Toxicology reports
  • Study documentation
05 Clinical

Clinical Study Reports

Clinical study information supporting the safety and efficacy evaluation of the product.

  • Clinical study reports
  • Clinical summaries
  • Safety information
  • Efficacy information

CTD structure and document examples are presented for orientation. Exact content and regional requirements depend on the applicable submission pathway and authority.

How it works

From regulatory change to informed review.

A connected workflow helps teams organize an update, understand where it may matter and document the review outcome.

01

Capture the regulatory change

Record the authority, source reference, publication date, effective date and relevant content of a guideline, notification or amendment.

INPUT · REGULATORY INTELLIGENCE
02

Identify potentially affected records

AI-assisted analysis compares the update with available product attributes and linked CTD documentation to surface possible relationships.

ANALYSIS · PRODUCT + DOSSIER MAPPING
03

Verify, assess and document

Regulatory professionals review the suggested matches, determine applicability, record rationale and identify any required follow-up.

OUTPUT · HUMAN-VERIFIED ASSESSMENT

ReguTrack is a pharmaceutical regulatory affairs platform concept that connects regulatory intelligence, product registration records, CTD dossier management and AI-assisted regulatory impact assessment in one workspace.

The CTD is a harmonized format for organizing information in regulatory submissions. It contains five modules covering regional administrative information, summaries, quality, nonclinical study reports and clinical study reports.

A regulatory update is recorded and analyzed against available product and dossier information. The system can surface potentially related products and documents for review. Final applicability and actions must be determined by a regulatory professional.
