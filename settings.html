Settings

Manage your profile, workspace preferences, notifications and regulatory sources.

Profile information

Update the details displayed in your ReguTrack workspace.

Workspace configuration

Set the organization details and default workspace region.

Notification preferences

Choose which workspace events should generate notifications.

Regulatory update alerts

Notify when a new regulatory update is added to the workspace.

Impact assessment reminders

Remind users about assessments awaiting an RA decision.

Calendar deadline reminders

Enable reminders for upcoming submissions and milestones.

CTD review notifications

Notify when dossier documents need review or action.

Regulatory source preferences

Select the authorities and standards relevant to your workspace.

These are workspace preferences only. Selecting a source does not establish a live connection or automatically retrieve official regulatory updates.

Data management

Export your saved preferences or reset this page to its default values.

Export settings
