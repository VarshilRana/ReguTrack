Role permissions

How the two roles are intended to operate within ReguTrack.

User

Works with assigned products and regulatory workflows.

  • View assigned products and CTD dossiers
  • Upload and manage dossier documents
  • Run gap analysis and view potential findings
  • Prepare impact assessments and calendar tasks
  • Submit review decisions when reviewer permission is granted

Admin

Manages workspace membership and has workspace-wide visibility.

  • View all products, dossiers and reports
  • Add, edit, deactivate and invite members
  • Assign User or Admin roles
  • Manage workspace settings and source configuration
  • Monitor activity, reviews and access
