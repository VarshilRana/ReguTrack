How a regulatory change becomes a verified product-level decision.

01
Capture the regulatory change

Record the authority, publication, revision and effective date using the official source.

02
Determine scope and applicability

Check jurisdiction, product type, application status and implementation conditions.

03
Map products and CTD documents

Identify candidate products and dossier sections that may require review, with supporting rationale.

04
Record RA decision and actions

Document whether the change applies, what action is needed, who owns it and its due date.
